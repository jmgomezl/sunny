What Sunny is

Sunny is an AI companion that checks tokens, links, wallets and Blinks on Solana, and can spend a small allowance from a "pocket" whose limits are enforced by a Solana program. It is an early, open-source project, provided as is.

Sunny gives security information to help you decide; it is not financial, investment or legal advice. Its checks and the AI's words can be incomplete or wrong. You are responsible for what you sign and send. Never share your recovery phrase or private key with anyone, including Sunny.

Test networks

The pocket, the Sunny wallet and test USDC run on Solana devnet and have no real value. Watched wallets and market data come from Solana mainnet and are read-only.

Your keys, your responsibility

A Sunny wallet is locked with a password only you know; there is no reset. If you sign in with your own wallet, that wallet stays yours and Sunny never holds its keys. Sunny's own spending key can only draw from your pocket within the limits you set, and nothing once you freeze it.
